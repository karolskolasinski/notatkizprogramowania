## Bootstrap breakpoints

| **Breakpoint**    | **Class** | **Dimensions** |
| ----------------- | --------- | -------------- |
| X-Small           | _none_    | \<576px        |
| Small             | sm        | ≥576px         |
| Medium            | md        | ≥768px         |
| Large             | lg        | ≥992px         |
| Extra large       | xl        | ≥1200px        |
| Extra extra large | xxl       | ≥1400px        |

[https://getbootstrap.com/docs/5.0/layout/breakpoints/#available-breakpoints](https://getbootstrap.com/docs/5.0/layout/breakpoints/#available-breakpoints)
